**JamLoom Community Edition is free to download and use.**

JamLoom 0.1.0 introduces a free Community Edition for turning chord charts into backing tracks.

- Arrange chord progressions into song sections and generate bass, drums, and guitar accompaniment from a selected style.
- Set the practice tempo, loop a passage, and balance the three accompaniment instruments.
- Export editable MIDI or a WAV backing track. The licensed edition adds portable project save and reopen.
